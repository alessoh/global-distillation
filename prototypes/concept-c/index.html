Six perspectives

The same phenomenon, read six ways

Each perspective is a separate data file with its own statistics, tables, charts and sources. The headline finding from each is summarised here.

Academic01

Distilled reasoners keep ~90% of teacher accuracy at 1/20 the parameters.

DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B scores 72.6 on AIME 2024 against the 671B-parameter teacher's 79.8 — evidence that reasoning traces transfer as supervised data alone.

91%AIME 2024 retention, 32B student vs. R1 teacher
Financial02

Small tiers are priced 15–20× below their frontier parent within a quarter.

GPT-4o output tokens list at $10.00 per million; GPT-4o mini at $0.60. The gap is the business model of distillation, and it compounds as frontier prices fall.

16.7×GPT-4o : GPT-4o mini output price
Political03

Regulators treat distillation as both a compliance path and a leakage risk.

The EU AI Act's general-purpose model obligations (in force 2 Aug 2025) reach downstream fine-tuners; US export-control debate frames distillation as capability transfer.

7jurisdictions with relevant provisions
Company04

Terms of service now name distillation explicitly — and enforcement is starting.

OpenAI alleged in January 2025 that DeepSeek used its outputs to train competing models, citing usage terms that prohibit using outputs "to develop models that compete".

5 / 6major labs restrict output-based training
Developer05

Managed distillation is a product: upload prompts, pick a student, pay per token.

OpenAI, Google and open-source stacks (TRL, DistilKit, Unsloth) turn a research technique into a form; the median recipe costs under $500 in teacher tokens.

$420median teacher-token cost per recipe
Customer06

For most production workloads the distilled tier is the rational default.

Latency, price and retention line up: the compendium's decision guide recommends a distilled or small-tier model for 9 of 12 canonical use cases.

9 / 12use cases where a distilled model is recommended

Timeline · Political · Company

Ten years, six turning points

Academic Financial Company Political

    Academic

    Where the retention ceiling sits

    Students above roughly 30B parameters recover more than nine-tenths of teacher performance on reasoning suites; below 7B the curve falls off quickly. The open question is whether on-policy objectives move that knee to the left.

    Political

    Whose outputs are they anyway

    The OpenAI–DeepSeek dispute turned a training technique into a trade question. Terms of service forbid training competitors on outputs; no court has yet said whether that clause is enforceable, or whether it matters once the weights are open.

    Company

    Every flagship now ships a shadow

    Of the fourteen frontier releases in the past year, twelve arrived with a smaller sibling within ninety days. The sibling, not the flagship, carries most of the traffic by the following quarter.

    Developer

    Distill your own for the price of an evaluation run

    Fine-tuning a 7B model on two hundred thousand teacher traces costs under $400 on rented hardware. The bottleneck has moved from compute to curating the prompt set.

    Customer

    What the end user notices

    Latency, mostly. In blind tests customers preferred the student for chat and the teacher for anything with a citation. Price rarely enters the comparison; it is decided upstream.

    Methodology

    • Retention = student score ÷ teacher score on the same benchmark and prompt format.
    • Prices are list, per 1M tokens, USD, without cached-input or batch discounts.
    • Open-weight prices are the median of three hosted providers.
    • Categories: research, product, policy, market, legal.
